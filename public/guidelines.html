Community Guidelines

Our goal is to create a fun and safe environment for everyone. Please follow these guidelines to help us maintain a positive community.

Be Respectful

Treat others as you would like to be treated. Do not engage in harassment, bullying, or hate speech. Respect differences in opinion, background, and identity.

Keep it Clean

Do not share sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise graphic content. Using obscene or abusive language is prohibited and will result in a disconnection after a warning.

Protect Your Privacy

This is an anonymous chat. For your own safety, **do not share personal information** such as your full name, address, phone number, social media accounts, or financial details.

No Illegal Activity

Do not use the service to conduct or promote any illegal activities. This includes, but is not limited to, selling illegal goods, phishing, or spreading malware.

Report Bad Behavior

If you encounter a user who is violating these guidelines, please use the "Report" button. While we cannot review all chats, reports help us identify and manage problematic behavior on the platform.
