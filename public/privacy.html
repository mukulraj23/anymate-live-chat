Introduction

Welcome to AnyMate. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and why.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our service is designed for anonymity. We do not require user accounts, registration, or personal information to use our chat service.

  • We **do not** collect personal names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We **do not** store chat messages after a chat session has ended. All messages are ephemeral and are deleted from server memory upon disconnection.
  • We **do not** use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Information We Collect Automatically

To operate and maintain the service, we may automatically collect some non-personal information:

  • IP Address: Your IP address is temporarily processed by our servers to establish and maintain a connection. We do not store IP addresses in a way that can be linked to your chat sessions.
  • Server Logs: Our servers may keep temporary, anonymous logs for technical troubleshooting and security purposes (e.g., to prevent DDoS attacks). These logs do not contain chat content.

How We Use Information

The limited information we process is used solely for the technical operation of the website, including pairing users, relaying messages, and ensuring the stability and security of our platform.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information processed by our service. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
